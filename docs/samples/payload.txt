TERMINOLOGY
  - business object: Also known as Initiative, Order, or BO. Represents a workflow process.
  - delayed task: A task whose elapsed duration exceeds its SLA buffer.
  - my tasks: Tasks assigned to the current user that are open and meant to be displayed.
BUSINESS RULES
  - active_business_object: An Active business object (Initiative, Order, BO) is one where business_object_status = 'Active'.
      SQL: tms_business_object_flat.business_object_status = 'Active'
  - short_closed_business_object: A Short Closed business object is one where business_object_status = 'Short Closed'.
      SQL: tms_business_object_flat.business_object_status = 'Short Closed'
  - closed_business_object: A Closed business object is one where business_object_status = 'Closed'.
      SQL: tms_business_object_flat.business_object_status = 'Closed'
  - open_task: An open task is one where task_status = 'open'.
      SQL: tms_task_flat.task_status = 'open'
  - closed_task: A closed task is one where task_status = 'closed'.
      SQL: tms_task_flat.task_status = 'closed'
  - delayed_task: A task is delayed when calculated elapsed duration exceeds its SLA buffer.
      SQL: tms_task_flat.task_sla_status = 'Delayed'
  - my_tasks: My tasks means tasks assigned to the current signed-in user, still open, and flagged for display. In this benchmark the signed-in user is user id 1 ('Admin'); use that literal id, never a bind placeholder.
      SQL: tms_task_flat.assigned_user_id = 1 AND tms_task_flat.task_status = 'open' AND tms_task_flat.display_flag = 1
      scope: The open + display_flag conditions apply only to the bare concept "my tasks". Do NOT add them when the question supplies its own status or asks for a distribution across statuses -- they would contradict or empty the answer. "my closed tasks" is assigned_user_id = 1 AND task_status = 'closed'. "my delayed tasks" is assigned_user_id = 1 AND task_sla_status = 'Delayed'. "my task status breakdown" is assigned_user_id = 1 GROUP BY task_status. The only invariant across all of them is assigned_user_id = 1.
  - prefer_pre_aggregated_counts: tms_business_object_flat already carries total_task_count, open_task_count, closed_task_count and delayed_task_count. When a question is about a Business Object's task counts, read these columns rather than joining to tms_task_flat and re-aggregating: it is the same answer from one table, and it keeps the choice deterministic instead of leaving two equally reasonable shapes.
      SQL: tms_business_object_flat.total_task_count
  - count_business_objects_from_tasks: When counting Business Objects from task data, use COUNT(DISTINCT bo_id) rather than COUNT(*).
      SQL: COUNT(DISTINCT tms_task_flat.bo_id)
  - entity_alias: Initiative, Order, BO and Business Object are all the same TMS entity (tms_business_object_flat).
      SQL: tms_business_object_flat
